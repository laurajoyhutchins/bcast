# Code of Conduct

BCAST welcomes rigorous technical collaboration conducted professionally and in good faith.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas, evidence, and implementation choices rather than attack people;
- distinguish documented facts from assumptions and interpretations;
- give review feedback that is specific enough to act on;
- respect source rights, confidentiality, and security boundaries;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexual harassment, doxxing, or deliberate disruption;
- stop publishing sensitive material if a maintainer identifies a security, privacy, or source-rights concern.

Technical disagreement is normal. Strong criticism of an approach is acceptable when it remains focused on the work and supported by reasons or evidence.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not escalate a personal or sensitive conduct concern through a public issue if doing so would expose private information. Contact the repository owner through an appropriate private channel.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when conduct materially interferes with safe, productive collaboration.

This policy governs participation in BCAST project spaces. It does not create authority over third-party communities or organizations.
